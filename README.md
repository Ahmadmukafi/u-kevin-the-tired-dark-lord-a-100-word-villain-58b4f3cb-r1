A 102-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired —
spoken by a dark lord with seven conquered kingdoms, a drafty east tower, and
lukewarm porridge. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small
deflections, never in a plain statement; the sting lands in the last line.

The program writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (102 words, 556 characters).
